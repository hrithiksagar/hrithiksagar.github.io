Selected Research

ECCV 2026

DoCoG: Mask-based Multi-Type Grounded Chain-of-Thought for Document QA

Sai Madhusudan*, Jyothi Swaroopa*, Hrithik Sagar*, et al. (*equal contribution)

Extends grounding beyond answers to the reasoning itself: every step of the chain-of-thought is grounded in textual and graphical document elements through a Grounding Interaction Module with dedicated grounding tokens — so you can see exactly where each reasoning step looks.

paper project page
CVPR 2026

M3Grounder: Mask-Based Multi-Span and Multi-Granular Grounding for Document QA

…, Jyothi Swaroopa†, Hrithik Sagar†, et al. (†equal contribution)

Mask-based grounding that handles answers scattered across multiple spans and granularities — words, lines, blocks, figures. Introduces GroundingDocQA: 200K documents, 2M QA pairs. State-of-the-art on DOGR-Bench, BoundingDocs, and MMDocBench.

project page paper dataset
Open Model · HuggingFace + IndiaAI AIKosh

Patram-7B: India's First Foundational Document VLM

BharatGen · Hrithik Sagar — synthetic data engine, multi-node training, benchmarking lead

Supports 22 Indian languages. Built the synthetic data engine that generated 10M QA pairs across 400K documents, ran multi-node training on 64 H100 GPUs, and led all benchmarking for the release.
